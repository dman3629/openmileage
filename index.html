Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Log, CSV and print export stay free. Pro adds extras.

Where is my data?

In your browser. Clear your browser data and it is gone unless you backed it up.

What do HMRC and the IRS want in a log?

Date, miles, purpose; odometer readings help. This log records all of them.

Can I move from Everlance, Driversnote, Stride or MileIQ?

Export a CSV from them and use Import.

Is it open source?

Yes: github.com/dman3629/openmileage
